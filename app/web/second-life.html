Second Life Electronics

The first specialised vertical of the directory: the repair, refurbishment, parts recovery, resale and recycling of the electronics that already exist in Kampala. It is two things at once — six subcategories of listings you can browse like any other part of the directory, and an intake book that follows a single device from the counter to a named outcome.

The six activities

Every activity is a real subcategory, so a listing filed under one is reachable through the directory as well as here. The list is read from the engine, so it cannot drift from the taxonomy it describes.

What the intake records that a listing cannot

A directory entry says a workshop exists. It cannot say that a particular phone came in cracked on Tuesday, was diagnosed, had its screen replaced, and left with its data wiped. An intake is that record. It carries the device, the condition it arrived in, the activity it is being handled under, and a reference of the form SL-2026-00042 that is quoted over the counter.

  1. Received The device is booked in against a business, with its make, model and condition.
  2. Diagnosed Somebody has looked at it and knows what is wrong or what it is worth in parts.
  3. In progress The work is happening: the repair, the refurbishment, the teardown.
  4. Ready It is finished and waiting to be collected, sold on or processed.
  5. Closed — with an outcome Repaired, refurbished, parts recovered, resold, recycled, returned or scrapped. An intake is never closed without saying which.

The book only ever moves forward, and it is only ever a business's own. An intake with an outstanding data-wipe request cannot be closed as resold, parts-recovered, recycled or returned until the wipe is recorded — the one promise in this vertical that is enforced rather than merely intended.

Endpoints

The activities list is public. Every other route is an operator read or write, because an intake names a specific device and who it belongs to.

  • /api/v1/second-life/activities Public. The six activities and the category each is filed under
  • POST /api/v1/second-life/businesses/:id/intakes Take a device in. Requires business:write
  • GET /api/v1/second-life/businesses/:id/intakes A business's intake book, filterable by stage, outcome and activity
  • GET /api/v1/second-life/businesses/:id/summary Counts by stage, outcome and activity, including wipes requested and done
  • GET /api/v1/second-life/intakes/:reference One intake, its history, and the stages it may move to next
  • POST /api/v1/second-life/intakes/:reference/advance Move an intake forward a stage. Backward moves are refused
  • POST /api/v1/second-life/intakes/:reference/close Close with an outcome. The data-wipe rule is applied here
  • POST /api/v1/second-life/intakes/:reference/wipe Record that a requested data wipe was completed
