Every number above is an aggregate over searches from many people. Anything computed over fewer than the number of distinct people this install requires is withheld rather than rounded, capped or approximated: the engine has the measurement, and it tells the caller the measurement is not publishable yet. A handful of searches cannot be subtracted back out of a total of three, so the floor is what makes the rest of this page worth reading.

The gaps, ranked

    A finding is a hypothesis with a stated reason, not a promise. The engine counts the demand, counts the supply that answers it, scores the difference and stores the sentence it wrote next to the number — so a conclusion stays explainable after the data underneath it has moved on. Following a gap's keyword runs a search in the directory, and like every search on this site that is recorded as a demand signal, from this page as much as from any other.
