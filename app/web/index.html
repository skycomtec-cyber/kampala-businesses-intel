Kampala Intelligence Platform

A reference directory of businesses in Kampala and the districts around it, and the demand engine that shows where the gaps are. The directory starts empty on purpose: it is a reference, not a scraper's guess, and the only way a listing gets in is somebody who was there entering it.

Endpoints

  • /api/v1/locations Every administrative level, filterable with ?kind=ward
  • /api/v1/categories The category tree, with the skills linked to each node
  • /api/v1/search?q=… Full-text search. Records an anonymised demand signal
  • /api/v1/insights/opportunities Ranked gaps, subject to the k-anonymity floor
  • /api/v1/requests What people are looking for, posted without an account
  • /api/v1/meta Version, configuration, limits and current counts

Aggregates computed over fewer than — distinct people are withheld rather than rounded, so a small number of searches can never be subtracted down to an individual. The engine knows the answer and says that it is not publishable yet.
